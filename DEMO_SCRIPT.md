## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Screen Recording Checklist
1. **Landing page** — hero + features scroll (5 sec)
2. **MetaMask connect** — wallet connection flow (3 sec)
3. **Dashboard empty state** — before deposit (3 sec)
4. **Deposit flow** — faucet → amount → approve → confirm (15 sec)
5. **Dashboard live state** — balance + allocation updated (5 sec)
6. **Chronicle tab** — empty → chapters appear (5 sec)
7. **SAGE chat** — ask question → response (8 sec)
8. **Risk Mood** — zoom on the pill indicator (3 sec)
9. **Reasoning Log** — signals display (5 sec)
10. **Share button** — share modal with options (3 sec)

### Audio
- Background music: calm, ambient electronic (low volume)
- Narrator: clear, measured pace, no rush
- No sound effects for UI interactions (keep it clean)

### Text Overlays
- Scene titles in Inter/Manrope font, white on dark
- Feature labels: `RISK MOOD`, `REASONING LOG`, `CHRONICLE`, etc.
- Contract addresses small at bottom for transparency

### B-Roll Suggestions
- Mantle ecosystem logos
- USDY / Ondo Finance branding (fair use)
- mETH liquid staking visualization
- ERC-8004 badge/icon
